Privacy

What we collect.

This website sets no cookies and runs no analytics or advertising scripts of its own. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may process visitor data under GitHub's own privacy statement. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the page loads.

If you email us, we use your message and contact details only to respond and to carry out any work you ask for. We do not sell personal data. To ask us to delete your correspondence, email buildwithcolours@gmail.com.
