About Colour

A materials company built on evidence.

Colour designs, makes and verifies engineered materials for hardware that runs hot and cannot afford a fragile supply chain.

Mission and vision

Materials should be engineered, not just extracted.

For centuries, civilisation has taken its materials from the ground and accepted the limits that came with it. We think the next era belongs to materials designed with precision, made in controlled laboratories, recycled where possible and measured at every step.

Atoms do not come from nothing, so we state our aim plainly: reduce dependence on virgin extraction, one verified material at a time.

Governance and disclosure

We disclose our interest, and invite challenge.

Colour has a commercial interest in the materials it studies. To limit bias we source every factual claim, label evidence, report limits and negative findings, name the patents and competitors that constrain us, and credit corrections.

Published: literature syntheses, teaching material, methods, open questions and, when we have it, our own results.

Not published: customer data and specifications, partner-confidential information and proprietary process details.

Our policies are public: governance, research integrity, contributing and security.
