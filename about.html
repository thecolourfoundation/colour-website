About

A materials company built on proof.

Colour is an early-stage advanced materials company. We design, make and test materials for hardware teams that run hot and cannot afford a fragile supply chain. Customers bring a specification. We return verified material and the data behind it.

01 / Why we exist

Materials should be engineered, not just extracted.

For centuries, civilisation has taken its materials from the ground and accepted the limits that came with it. We think the next era belongs to materials designed with precision, made in controlled laboratories, recycled where possible and measured at every step.

Atoms do not come from nothing, so we are plain about our aim: reduce dependence on virgin extraction, one verified material at a time.
