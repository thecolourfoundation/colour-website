Approach

How a specification becomes a verified material.

Materials work is usually split across suppliers, laboratories and consultants, and every handoff costs time. We organise around a single recorded loop and an independent check at the end.

The loop

Specify, design, make, verify.

01

Specify

Written, measurable fields agreed before any work starts, with acceptance criteria.

02

Design

Experiments chosen from literature, simulation and the record of past runs.

03

Make and measure

The same methods every time, with calibrated instruments and a batch record for every sample.

04

Verify

Independent testing, then release with a datasheet. Results are reported against a baseline, misses included.

Commitments

What customers can rely on.

  • Confidentiality. Customer specifications and data are never shared with other customers.
  • Ownership. Your designs remain yours. Our core processes remain ours. Jointly developed work is agreed in writing beforehand.
  • Neutrality. We make materials to your specification and do not compete with your products.
  • Honest reporting. Anything not yet measured is labelled a target.

Data stewardship

Every run is recorded.

Each sample is linked to its inputs, settings, operator and measurements, so results can be traced and compared. Our private run record is part of how we get faster, and it stays private. What we publish are methods, summaries and independent test reports, where our partners permit.

Facilities and partners

Where we are today.

We do not yet operate a laboratory. We are in discussions with external laboratories and materials suppliers for characterisation and feedstock, and we will name partners when agreements are in place.
